## Subtitle

## Description
Wake up on time for school - but only when there IS school.

School Alarm automatically syncs with your school district's calendar to skip alarms on holidays, breaks, and non-school days. No more waking up early on Christmas morning or during spring break.

**Features:**
- Automatic holiday detection - alarms skip on school holidays and breaks
- SFUSD calendar support - syncs with San Francisco Unified School District schedule
- Custom alarm sounds - choose from built-in sounds or import your own
- Weekly schedule rules - set different times for different days
- One-time overrides - easily skip or reschedule individual days
- Snooze support - because sometimes you need 5 more minutes

**Perfect for:**
- Parents managing school morning routines
- Students who want to sleep in on holidays
- Families tired of manually disabling alarms before every break

Simple to set up, works automatically. Just pick your district, set your alarm time, and let the app handle the rest.
