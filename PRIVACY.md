# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 31, 2026

## Overview

School Alarm is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device:
- Alarm settings and times
- Selected school district
- Schedule overrides and custom rules
- Sound preferences

This data never leaves your device.

## Network Access

The app fetches publicly available school district calendar data (ICS feeds) to determine school days and holidays. No personal information is sent in these requests.

## Third-Party Services

School Alarm does not use any analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party data collection services.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at:
https://github.com/thinkingfish/schoolAlarm/issues

## Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
